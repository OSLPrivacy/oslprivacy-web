This is how protected text works today, verified on QA builds and not yet on the release build. The connected service stores the cover message and the encrypted item; it never receives the readable text. It does still see who you talked to and when. Link protection Planned

Share the link. Lose the trackers.

Planned. There is no tracker-stripping code in OSL yet, so links you send today are unchanged. The drawing below shows the intended behaviour: strip the tracking parameters from a link before it is shared.

Scrub Planned Planned

Find what you left behind.

The scan is designed to stay on your device.

Planned, and not in the shipping app yet. Scrub is designed to find the accounts and the exposure you left behind, then take you to the page where you delete it. It will not erase anything for you: you stay the one who confirms every deletion, on the service's own page. The guided handoff can fail on a service that changes its pages. Expiry Planned

Readable until expiry.

Planned: set how long a message stays readable in OSL.

Planned, and not wired into the app yet. When it does ship it will stop OSL from showing the readable message after the timer ends, leaving the cover text in place. It will not destroy a key, and it cannot make a copy, screenshot, export or backup that already left your device expire. The drawing below shows the intended behaviour.

Before-send warning

Pause before it leaves.

OSL catches a sensitive draft before it reaches an unencrypted box.

The check runs on your own machine and your draft never leaves it. The wording is plain about consequences rather than moral, and the decision is always yours — OSL pauses, it does not refuse. Arriving at v1.

Link protection

Share the link. Lose the trackers.

OSL strips the tracking parameters out of a link before you share it, so the person who receives it cannot be followed back to you.

Scrub

Find what you left behind.

The scan stays on your device.

Years of forgotten accounts, old posts and stale logins are still out there under your name. Scrub goes looking for them on your own machine — nothing is uploaded to find them.

Then it takes you to the page where you delete each one. Scrub never deletes on your behalf: you confirm every removal yourself, on the service's own page, and OSL only reports a deletion once the service confirms it. Arriving at v1.

Attachment Privacy Guard

Clean first.
Lock second.

A photo carries more than the picture: where it was taken, which device took it, and when. OSL strips those hidden details out first, then encrypts what is left.

Arriving at v1, along with view once, which lets a picture be opened a single time. Until then, assume a file you send still carries its original details.

Expiry

Readable until expiry.

Set how long a message stays readable in OSL. When the timer ends, OSL stops showing the readable text and what remains in the app is the encrypted item.

Worth being precise about what a timer can and cannot do: it stops OSL from showing you the message. It does not destroy a key, and it cannot reach a copy, screenshot, export or backup that already left your device. Arriving at v1.

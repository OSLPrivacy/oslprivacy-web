Harmless carrier
Locked real message
  1. You type in the clearly marked OSL composer above the normal service send bar.
  2. You approve readable cover text; your device adds a token and encrypts the separate real message locally.
  3. The service gets the readable cover and token. The OSL relay gets a blob it cannot unlock.
  4. Your friend's device combines both paths and restores the real message over the cover.

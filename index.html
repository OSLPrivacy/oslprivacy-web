The honest limits

Encryption does not make you invisible.

Services still see activity

A connected service can still see your account, recipients, groups, timing, and encrypted message data.

Your device still matters

OSL cannot protect a hacked computer, a camera, a recipient screenshot, or text sent in normal mode.

Both people need OSL

A private chat works only after every required person installs OSL and approves it.

Voice and video are separate

OSL protects selected text and supported files. Calls still use the connected service's normal system.

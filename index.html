Message protection Beta

Your words never
reach their server.

You write in OSL on your own computer. OSL encrypts it there, and only the encrypted form is handed to the social or email app — your readable words never reach their server. Your friend's copy of OSL turns it back into the real message on their computer. Today that encrypted form is visibly an encrypted block rather than an innocuous-looking message; disguising it as ordinary text is arriving at v1. The service also still sees who you messaged and when. Read how the encryption works.

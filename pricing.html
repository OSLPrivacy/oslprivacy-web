Questions

Is this a test checkout?

If Stripe checkout opens, it is a live payment page. Completing it creates a real charge.

Will you email the activation code?

No. The code appears in the browser that started checkout. Stripe may ask for billing details on its hosted page, but OSL does not store the returned email for this purchase.

Does it renew?

No. The current Pro checkout is a one-time $5 purchase. It does not start a recurring payment.

What payment data does OSL keep?

OSL does not copy your card details or Stripe email into its product database. Stripe keeps the payment record required to process the purchase.

Does Pro send encrypted files today?

No. The purchase activates Pro beta supporter access. Cross-device protected messages, encrypted attachment transport, and automated Scrub are not enabled in version 0.0.1.

Can I inspect the payment code?

Yes. The site and payment backend are public on GitHub.

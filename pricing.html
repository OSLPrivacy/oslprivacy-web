How the free tier works

OSL Free is free because of ads. Every 60 minutes of active use, OSL prompts you to take a short break — your browser opens a page on oslprivacy.com, plays a 60-second ad, and sends you back to OSL with another 60 minutes unlocked.

Ads only run on our website. We don't embed ad SDKs inside OSL itself — the OSL client never talks to ad networks. This keeps your messaging surface clean and means ad-network tracking can't reach into your conversations.

The 60-minute timer only counts active use. If OSL is in the background or minimized, the timer pauses. Light users may never hit the threshold; heavy users see a few ad breaks per day.

If you'd rather skip the ad breaks entirely, the Paid tier is $5/month or $50/year and removes them.

Pricing FAQ

Why charge at all? OSL is open source.

Running OSL needs servers, code-signing certificates, and development time. Paid users keep the project independent.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit card via Stripe, Bitcoin, and Monero. Crypto payments provide stronger privacy at checkout.

What ads will I see on the Free tier?

We'll publish the exact ad provider before the Free tier goes live. Whatever we pick, ads will be contextual to oslprivacy.com, not personalized to you. The ad network sees your IP and browser the same way any website visitor would.

Can I block the ads with uBlock Origin or similar?

Technically yes — they're served from our website. But blocking ads doesn't extend your OSL timer. The unlock comes from completing the ad-watch flow on our server, not from the ad itself loading. If you don't want to see ads, the Paid tier ($5/month) is the supported path.

What happens if I cancel my paid subscription?

You go back to the Free tier with ad breaks. Your encrypted data, keys, and history stay intact on your computer.

How the free tier works

The free tier isn't a trial. It's not a limited preview. Encrypted text messaging works the same on both tiers — no time limits, no message caps, no ads. We make money when someone wants to send encrypted attachments, which is the heavier feature to support. Receivers of encrypted attachments don't pay — so a free user can read what a paid friend sends them.

What we never charge for

End-to-end encryption

Every tier gets the same cryptographic protection — Free and Paid users have identical security.

Your data

Your keys and messages live on your computer. We can't read them. There's nothing to sell, even if we wanted to.

Your contact list

OSL never builds a social graph of who talks to whom. Discord sees that metadata, we don't.

Pricing FAQ

Why charge at all? OSL is open source.

Running OSL needs servers, code-signing certificates, and development time. Paid users keep the project independent.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit card via Stripe, Bitcoin, and Monero. Crypto payments provide stronger privacy at checkout.

What happens if I cancel my paid subscription?

You go back to the free tier — unlimited encrypted text messaging stays available, you just lose the ability to send encrypted attachments and beta channel access until you resubscribe.

Payment processing ships at launch. Stripe, Bitcoin, and Monero supported. Sign up to be first to know.

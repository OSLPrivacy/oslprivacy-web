Ad disclosure

How OSL's free-tier ads work, and what data is involved.

The mechanic #

Every 60 minutes of active foreground use, OSL prompts you to watch a 60-second ad break. The ad opens in your default browser on oslprivacy.com/watch. When the ad completes, OSL unlocks for another 60 minutes.

The 60-minute timer only counts active use. If OSL is in the background or minimized, the timer pauses. Light users may never hit the threshold; heavy users see a few ad breaks per day.

What data is involved #

The ad network sees: your IP address, browser fingerprint, operating system — the same information any website visitor exposes.

The ad network does NOT see: your Discord activity, your messages, your contacts, your encryption keys, or anything else from inside OSL. The OSL client never talks to ad networks.

When the ad completes, your browser sends an unlock token back to OSL. The token is a random one-time code — not tied to your Discord identity, your email, or any persistent identifier. OSL accepts the token and resets the timer.

Why we chose this model #

Most apps that run ads embed an ad SDK that tracks users continuously — across sessions, across applications, sometimes across devices. The SDK lives inside the app and has the same level of access to your data as the app does.

We didn't want that. The OSL client has no ad SDK and no ad-network connections. Ads only run on our website, where the privacy implications are visible and bounded: it's a website visit, the ad network sees what they would see if you visited any website with ads.

You can also pay $5/month or $50/year to remove ads entirely. See pricing.

The ad network #

We'll publish the exact ad provider here before the free tier goes live. Whatever we pick, ads will be contextual to oslprivacy.com, not personalized to you.

Blocking ads #

You can block ads with browser extensions like uBlock Origin. The ads are served from our website, so any standard ad blocker will stop them from loading.

Blocking ads will not extend your OSL timer. The unlock token comes from completing the ad-watch flow on our server, not from the ad itself loading. You'll need to load the watch page (with or without the ad rendering) to get a fresh 60 minutes.

If you don't want to see ads, the supported path is the paid tier ($5/month). It's $5 in exchange for never seeing an ad break.

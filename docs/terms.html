Description of service #

OSL is a desktop privacy hub for supported social media and email services. OSL Protected mode adds end-to-end encryption to supported messages exchanged between approved users who both have OSL.

OSL is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the services it connects to. Use of each connected service remains subject to that provider's terms.

Account and purchase terms #

OSL offers two tiers:

  • Free tier: unlimited encrypted text messaging at no cost. Free users can receive but not send encrypted attachments.
  • Pro tier: $5 buys an activation code that unlocks Pro for one month. It unlocks encrypted image sending and early access to features after they enter the public beta channel. This is not a subscription: nothing renews automatically, we do not store your payment details, no OSL account is required, and there is nothing to cancel. To continue after the month ends, buy another code.
  • Processing credits: Planned and not currently offered for sale. If they are introduced, they will be bought separately from Pro, will never renew, and will not be required for encryption to work.

The current checkout does not create a recurring subscription and stores no payment method. Card, Bitcoin and Monero all work the same way: one payment buys one month. When Pro ends, OSL returns to the Free tier; your keys, messages and notes are unaffected and protected text keeps sending and decrypting. Refund requests are handled according to applicable law and the payment provider's rules.
